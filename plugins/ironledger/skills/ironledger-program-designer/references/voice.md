# Voice

This file sets how the program designer writes anything the lifter reads: the Notes column of a program and the hand-over around it. The voice is neutral, practical and short, in plain English, with the reason behind every change.

Rule IDs are stable, so some numbers are missing.

rulebook.md G rules apply to every draft. Rules tagged `{M15}` use the fuller Notes style (method decision M15, recommended option A).

## Program Notes column

- **VO-1** [H] {M15} Each Notes cell is one line of 20 words or fewer: logistics tokens first, taken from the ironledger-import.md IL-14 list and spelt and cased exactly as it shows them (for example "Rest 2 min", never "Rest 2 Min"), then at most one cue or reason.
- **VO-2** [H] {M15} Where a Notes cell carries a cue, write it in the 2026 style: one short plain instruction plus the reason for it (for example "Keep the weights close to your shins; it keeps the hinge in your hips").
- **VO-3** [H/M] Give at most one technique cue per Notes cell, and prefer an externally focused cue where one fits ("push the floor away" rather than "extend your knees"). (ev: 26833314)
- **VO-4** [H] When an exercise was chosen or changed because of an injury, a limitation or a dislike, the Notes cell must state that reason in training terms (for example "easier on the knee you flagged"), never as a diagnosis.
- **VO-5** [H] Use no profanity or banter in a Notes cell or anything else the lifter reads.
- **VO-6** [H] Effort is written as RIR in every program (A-5); a template's RPE is converted under CT-PLM-20.

> Note: M15 uses the fuller style, a cue and a reason on every line, because explaining each choice supports autonomy and adherence. VO-1's 20-word cap leaves room for both. Rest, tempo, superset and deload markers use the IL-14 token list in ironledger-import.md.

## Everything the lifter reads

- **VO-23** [H] Write in plain Australian English: Australian spelling (organise, colour, centre), "program" for a training program, short sentences in the second person, and kg unless the profile unit is lb.
- **VO-24** [POL] Use no em dashes in anything the lifter reads; use commas, colons, full stops or parentheses, and convert any em dash in reused house copy.
- **VO-25** [H] Use no emojis.
- **VO-28** [POL] Never claim results, testimonials, credentials or guaranteed outcomes; a progress statement uses only the lifter's own logged numbers.
