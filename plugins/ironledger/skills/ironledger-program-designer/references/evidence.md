# Evidence register

This register lists every research source the program designer may cite, and what each one actually supports. The skill cites research only from §1 of this file, and never from a blog or from memory. A new source is added here only after a PubMed check. §2 lists the official and practitioner sources behind policy and practice rules, and §3 lists what must never be cited.

Built 2026-09-23, with every entry checked on PubMed. Rule IDs are stable: never renumber; retire a rule with ~~strikethrough~~ and a dated note.

> Note: Tag is the grade of the finding a paper backs: S strong, M moderate, W weak. Where sources disagree, or a grade is mixed (e.g. weak-moderate), the lower grade is used. Papers verified without a grade, or that back only practice-based rules, are graded here from study design and never above M (meta-analysis, systematic review, consensus or large RCT: M; anything else: W); their Supports text ends "Grade from design".

> Note: Supports gives the claim and the numbers the paper verifiably backs. Where a secondary source misused a paper, Supports says what the paper does not show. "Full text" marks figures read beyond the abstract. First author and year are as PubMed lists them; the year may be the epub year where print is a year later.
